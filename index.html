      First time voting?

      A ballot is the list of things you vote on — candidates for office, plus measures (also called propositions or amendments) where you vote yes or no on a policy question.

      Each item on this site has a plain-language summary so you know what you're actually deciding, before you walk into the voting booth.

      Search for your county or zip code to see your specific ballot. Nothing here is your actual vote — it's a review tool to help you prepare.
